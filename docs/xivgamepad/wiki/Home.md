# XIVGamepad

XIVGamepad recreates the Final Fantasy XIV **cross hotbar** in FFXI. Hold a trigger and the HUD
lights up one half of your active hotbar set; press a d-pad direction or face button and that slot
fires. Double-taps and paddle combos open extra bars (WXHB and Expanded), just like XIV.

What to expect:

- **Hold-only controls.** Every gesture is "hold a modifier, press a button". Nothing toggles on a
  bare trigger press, so there is no mode to get lost in.
- **Native feel preserved.** Your left stick still moves you, the right stick still pans the
  camera, and the bare d-pad still selects party and alliance members — those never pass through
  the addon.
- **Menus stay controller-friendly.** Bare A confirms, bare B cancels, START opens the main menu,
  BACK focuses the active window — even while a game menu or the chat line is open.
- **Setup happens once.** A Steam Input profile shapes the controller, FFXI's own gamepad config
  owns movement/camera/targeting, and the in-game Key-Capture Wizard records the mapping on first
  login.
